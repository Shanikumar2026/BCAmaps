Step-by-step: stay away from distraction

Tick each step when you have set it up, not when you have read it. Your progress is saved in this browser.

    Focus timer

    One block, one task. Phone in another room before you press start.

    50:00

    More focus methods

    Eleven methods, each tagged by what it fixes: Start, Stay, Retain, Plan or Body. Open one, try it for a week, keep what works. Studies are named where one exists.

    Focus ladder: grow your block length

    No one has shown a quick way to raise raw attention span. What you can build is the habit of staying with one task for longer. Start below your limit and add time each week. Do 2 blocks a day with a 5-minute break between them, phone in another room, and move up only after finishing every block of the week before.

    Focus game: tap the code, ignore the noise

    Tiles flash for less than a second. Tap only the dark CODE tiles. Yellow tiles (Reels, Chat, Ads) are distractions: tapping one costs you a point. The tiles get faster as the round goes on.

    Tap CODE. Ignore the yellow tiles.

    Use it as a 30-second warm-up before a study block. Reviews of brain-training games find little transfer to real-world skills (Simons et al., 2016), so it shows your attention right now; it does not train it for good.
